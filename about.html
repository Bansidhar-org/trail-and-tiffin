Our story

It started with a packed lunch.

Trail & Tiffin is a fictional Bengaluru outdoor shop inspired by a familiar weekend rhythm: an early start, a walk into the hills and a tiffin shared at the top.

We built the range around the parts of the trip that matter most: carrying comfortably, eating well and settling in for the night.

Explore the range →

Our mission

Make weekend trekking easier to get into through a small, practical range of gear and clear prices in rupees.

Our vision

Help more first-time hikers feel at home on the trails around South India and learn how to look after them.

What makes us different

We put compact camp cookware alongside trail and shelter essentials, so you can plan the hike and the meal in one place.
